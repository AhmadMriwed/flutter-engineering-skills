# Import reference knowledge

1. Identify a readable source and pin its revision in the [source registry](../knowledge-management/SOURCE_REGISTRY.md). Record dirty or generated exclusions. Inspect dependencies and trace multiple representative runtime paths from entry to transport and back. Keep source files read-only.
2. Record findings with source-relative anchors in the [evidence registry](../knowledge-management/EVIDENCE_REGISTRY.md). Record absences and exceptions. Remove proprietary examples and sensitive values.
3. Classify and compare according to [skill evolution](../knowledge-management/SKILL_EVOLUTION.md). Determine whether the difference is a shared-standard candidate, profile-specific candidate, alternative, conflict or regression. Test compatibility of full combinations separately.
4. Present a proposal listing evidence, strengths, tradeoffs, compatibility, edits, retained rules, validation gaps and version impact. Obtain explicit approval for substantive adoption or replacement.
5. Apply approved edits only. Keep IDs stable, update catalogs, compatibility and evidence, and rerun links and portability checks. Record what was observed, tested or remains pending.
